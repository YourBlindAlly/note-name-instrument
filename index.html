Computer keyboard

Used when no MIDI keyboard is connected, or alongside one. Screen reader users: the playing area switches your screen reader to pass keys through, so tab to it after pressing Start.

  • A S D F G H J K L ; ' play white keys, starting on C.
  • W E T Y U O P play the black keys between them.
  • Z moves down an octave and X moves up. It starts with A on C3.

About the range

The sung samples cover F sharp 2 up to middle C (C4). A note outside that range plays a short beep at its true pitch instead of a sung name.
